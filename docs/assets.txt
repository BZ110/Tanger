TANGER — ASSET PROVENANCE

All supplied game content is original. No reference-game models, animation,
textures, recordings, locations, or UI were extracted or downloaded.

MODELS

public/assets/models/training-room.glb
public/assets/models/operator.glb
public/assets/models/training-unit.glb

Authored by scripts/build-assets.ts from smooth geometry, original material
choices, and an articulated original operator hierarchy. Rebuild with:
  npm run assets:build

The authoring script exports GLBs using Three.js GLTFExporter. Repeated room
parts share geometry and use GPU instancing. The script does not overwrite the
editable area manifest. These are first-pass original art assets, not AAA models.
Three.js and its addons are used under the dependency's MIT license.
Reference documentation: https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFExporter.html

SURFACE FINISH AND AUDIO

src/adapters/three/surface-finish.ts creates deterministic original surface grain
and scratches. src/adapters/browser/audio.ts synthesizes original short sound
effects. No downloaded textures, font files, music, or sound effects are used.

TITLE ART

Saved asset: public/assets/art/title-city.png
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool (imagegen skill), without reference
images. Used as title/loading screen artwork, not as a promised playable city.
Final prompt, verbatim:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: widescreen title-screen background artwork
for an original browser action RPG called TANGER, with typography added separately
by the application. Primary request: a polished, cinematic original cyberpunk
industrial district at blue hour after rain. Wide 16:9 composition: lower
pedestrian service platform in the foreground, monumental layered concrete and
metal towers receding into violet haze, a single strong warm red neon vertical
panel on the right, subtle cyan strip lights, overhead utilities, wet reflective
surfaces and highly detailed materials. The left third stays dark, calm and
low-detail for menu text. Style: sophisticated anime-influenced cinematic
environment painting with believable architecture and smooth detailed rendering,
not flat polygonal or low-poly art. Charcoal and steel-blue base, restrained
red-orange and cyan accents. No people, no readable words, no logos, no watermark.
Do not reproduce any existing game location, franchise iconography, characters,
or UI. This is original artwork drawing only on broad cyberpunk atmosphere.

CHARACTER PIPELINE TEST DATA

tests/fixtures/art-pipeline.glb is a technical bent-strip fixture generated with
tools/art/blender/fixture.py in official Blender 4.5.14 LTS. It contains original
test geometry, a two-joint rig, two short NLA clips, one test morph and an original
64px checker texture. It is not
a character model, a replacement operator, or an example of the desired anime
art quality. tests/fixtures/art-character.json records its test contract.
Ignored art/.build/ holds regenerable .blend/GLB/renders/reports for this fixture.

No third-party art was added for this workspace. Blender is GPL software, installed
locally from its official checksum-verified distribution, not committed. Its
exporter generates glTF data; tooling uses the Khronos glTF Validator under
Apache-2.0. Reference documentation is linked in art/RUNTIME.md. Future characters
must carry their own editable source, input provenance, and honest visual review.

BLENDER ARTIST TOOL LAYER / BEBBEN SOURCE

Original source resides in art/characters/bebben/source/bebben.blend, with editable
master textures, directional face masks, a shader profile, checkpoints and semantic
regions. The previous all-character initializer is archived and refuses execution.
Bebben remains working art, globally blocked at the face stage; no premium-quality,
production rig, runtime export or owner design approval is claimed.

Original localized chin, cheek and eyelid/eye seating corrections were made through
tools/art/blender/artist/, with actual before/after and accepted/rejected/partial
reviews under review/artist/. The tool renders transient diagnostic materials and
outlines, never extracted game content. A solved local lash example is stored in
art/examples/face/seated-eye-lash-corner; art/library/index.json labels reusable
Bebben pieces as working, not reviewed production bases. All geometry/textures in
this delivery are original TANGER work; no game models, texture dumps, unlicensed
base meshes or proprietary implementations were used. Public references are
educational/viewing references recorded in art/RUNTIME.md.
