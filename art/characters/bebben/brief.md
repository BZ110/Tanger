# Bebben — original character brief

Bebben is a friendly young adult for TANGER. Owner-fixed choices: short messy dark hair, brown eyes, a charcoal utility jacket, cargo trousers, boots, one small orange accent, simple silhouette, carefully modeled face and layered hair. Deliver a separate lab character with editable Blender source, a skinned GLB, blink and smile expressions and an idle; gameplay integration is outside this task.

## Authored decisions

Approximately 1.82 m including hair. Slim, relaxed proportions with a softly tapered jaw, broad cheek planes, a modeled nasal bridge and tip, narrow lips, open almond eyes and warm brown radial irises. The face is a continuous front/back quad surface with actual orbital apertures. Upper and lower lids, upper lashes and eye surfaces share unilateral blink keys. Smile raises and widens the mouth corners with a small cheek lift.

Hair uses a fitted underlayer beneath separate broad flattened crown, fringe, temple and nape locks. The part sits left of center; uneven fringe tips leave the eyes readable. Two restrained crown wisps establish messiness without a noisy outline.

A cropped charcoal utility jacket has a stand collar, ribbed hem/cuffs, front zipper, chest patch pockets with flaps, lower welt openings, back yoke and sleeve seams. Slate cargo trousers use tapered articulated knees, bellows thigh pockets and outside seams. Dark lace-up boots have shaped vamps, toe seams, layered outsoles and collars. A single orange sewn pull tab sits on the left upper sleeve. No luminous armor or oversized props.

## Surfaces and motion

Original reproducible texture paint: 2048 px skin with restrained cheek warmth; 1024 px directional hair artwork, separate jacket/cargo weave artwork, and a radial brown iris. External PNG masters are also packed into Blender and embedded in GLB. Standard glTF PBR is the portable baseline. There is no production face SDF or outline shader.

The meter-scaled source faces Blender -Y with soles at Z=0. An A-pose separates elbows and hands from the torso. Authored normalized weights connect clothes to the deform skeleton. Individual finger joints remain editable. A four-second in-place idle uses gentle breathing and coordinated head/shoulder arcs; no foot translation. Diagnostic poses are retained as fake-user source actions outside exported NLA tracks. They are inspection aids, not gameplay clips.
